## 🎯 Experience Level Differences

### 0-1 Years (Entry-level/Junior)
- **Salary**: $50K-$90K (US)
- **Focus**: Fundamentals, basic frameworks, simple CRUD
- **Interview**: Coding basics, framework syntax, simple problems
- **Companies**: Startups, internships, junior roles
- **Difficulty**: 40% easy, 50% medium, 10% hard

### 1-3 Years (Mid-level)
- **Salary**: $80K-$130K (US)
- **Focus**: Practical skills, testing, debugging, production
- **Interview**: Framework knowledge, algorithms, design basics
- **Companies**: Startups, Series A-C, Product companies
- **Difficulty**: 20% easy, 60% medium, 20% hard

### 3-5 Years (Senior)
- **Salary**: $120K-$180K (US)
- **Focus**: Architecture basics, performance, mentoring
- **Interview**: System design, architecture, optimization
- **Companies**: Scale-ups, mid-size, FAANG (L4/L5)
- **Difficulty**: 10% easy, 40% medium, 50% hard

### 5+ Years (Staff/Principal)
- **Salary**: $150K-$250K+ (US)
- **Focus**: Distributed systems, architecture, leadership
- **Interview**: Complex system design, scale, trade-offs
- **Companies**: FAANG, Unicorns, Large enterprises
- **Difficulty**: 5% easy, 30% medium, 65% hard
